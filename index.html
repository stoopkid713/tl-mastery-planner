Weapon Mastery in 60 seconds

Each weapon has its own mastery level from 0 to 200. Levels are bought with mastery XP, generated by burning Abyssal Contract tokens while killing mobs. Maxing one weapon costs roughly 1.18 billion XP total — but per-level cost grows steeply: L0→1 ≈ 590k, L199→200 ≈ 19.3M.

Mastery bonuses are global — they apply across every build that uses that weapon. So most players want every weapon high. The real question is how to spend your weekly tokens to get there fastest.

You don't have to burn the week you generate — tokens stack in inventory. Best practice: queue 20–40k tokens, light your full buff stack (food = 30 min duration), and burn one session in a single window.

Direct apply vs. stored XP▼

When you burn a token, you choose where the XP goes:

Direct apply — XP goes straight to your currently equipped weapon. Linear, no inventory clutter, can't redirect once spent.

Store to pool — XP banks in inventory as a "mastery XP item" you spend on any weapon later. Trades a small inventory cost for flexibility — farm with your strongest weapon while leveling a weaker one.

XP per token is identical either way. The choice is purely about which weapon ends up with the XP. Weapons already at L200 can only generate stored XP (or it's wasted).

This tool assumes direct-apply when showing "levels gained" — the math is the same regardless, the pool just delays where the levels land.

How buffs stack▼

Two separate multipliers compound:

XP per token = Base × (1 + Mastery %) × (1 + Efficiency %)

Mastery % increases the XP each kill generates. Sources: Amitoi panel, Mastery Report, Guild Blessing, Food, Black Anvil weapon.

Efficiency % decreases how many tokens each kill consumes — same XP per kill, more kills per token. Sources: Amitoi, Food.

All mastery sources add linearly, then multiply against total efficiency. A full stack vs Amitoi-only ≈ 2.6× XP per token.

Base XP per token = 638.7 measured on Mutant Slaughterers (31,935 XP from 50 base tokens, source video). Different zones may pay slightly different bases — override on the Session tab if you measure something different.

Where do these buffs come from?▼

Amitoi Effects: the in-game Amitoi panel shows rolled-up bonuses from collected Amitoi. Two columns appear there ("Amount Effects" and "Pal Synergy") — those are quest progression milestones. The right column is the cap you reach when both quests are complete. Read the final rolled-up mastery / efficiency numbers off the panel and type them into the card above.

Mastery Report: consumed when you use a token; gives a flat +30% Mastery for 30 minutes. Limited event item — save them for full-stack burns.

Guild Blessing Lv.3: 24-hour +10% Mastery buff. Costs Sollant + Rock to craft, requires Lv.3 guild facility.

Golden Apple Pie: 30 min food, the strongest single buff at +50% Mastery / +10% Efficiency. Always eat one before a burn session.

Black Anvil Weapon: passive +50% Mastery while wielding the Black Anvil variant. Doesn't stack across multiple Black Anvil weapons in your loadout.

Why 80k tokens / week?▼

Empirical average across active players. Token sources:

• Abyssal Contract daily login — flat per-day stipend

• Daily quests + co-op dungeons — burst rewards

• Open-world activities — bonus tokens from world events / chests

• Weekly cap rollover — unspent tokens carry forward

Hardcore players push higher (120k+) by hitting every daily and farming weekend events. Casual rotations land closer to 40–60k. Adjust the field above to your actual generation rate.

Per typical burn session: 20–40k tokens consumed in ~30 minutes. With food (30 min duration), you usually fit one full buffed burn before re-eating. Mastery Report tokens get consumed inside the session — limit factor for stacking that buff.

What does each buff actually save you?▼

Looking at a 20,000-token session at Amitoi default (5.1 / 7.0):

• Bare = 718 XP/token, ~14.4M total

• + Golden Apple Pie = 1,159 XP/token, ~23.2M total — +61% over bare

• + Black Anvil = 1,533 XP/token, ~30.7M total — +113%

• + Mastery Report = 1,757 XP/token, ~35.1M total — +145%

• Full stack (incl. Guild Blessing) = 1,892 XP/token, ~37.8M total — +163%

Each buff adds less marginal value than the one before it (diminishing returns from linear stacking). Food alone is by far the biggest single contributor — never burn without it.

The cost curve is independent of weapon type. Greatsword L130 costs the same XP as Longbow L130 — verified across all 9 weapon families. Quartic polynomial fitted to 23 measured anchor points. Max residual ±0.077%.

The model. Per-level XP cost is a quartic polynomial f(L) fitted by least-squares to 23 in-game measurements spanning L50 to L174. Max residual ±0.077% across all measured points. Verified weapon-type independent (Longbow + Spear at same level = identical cost).
Core Formulas
f(L) = c₄L⁴ + c₃L³ + c₂L² + c₁L + c₀
C(L) = Σ f(N) for N = 0 to L-1
= cumulative XP to reach level L
Mastery XP per kill = Base_Mob_XP × (1 + Total_Mastery%)
Tokens / kill ≈ floor(Base_Tokens / (1 + Efficiency%))
XP / token = 638.7 × (1 + mastery%) × (1 + efficiency%)
Example — Amitoi only (5.1% / 7.0%): 638.7 × 1.051 × 1.07 = 718 XP/token
Example — Full stack (150% / 18.5%): 638.7 × 2.5 × 1.185 = 1,892 XP/token

Anchor zones (the measured data)▼

23 in-game screenshots of weapon mastery XP costs were taken at:

• L50–L54 on Greatsword

• L80–L84 on Greatsword

• L100–L103 on Daggers

• L130–L134 on Longbow

• L150–L155 on Longbow (spot-test confirmation)

• L170–L174 on Spear

Longbow and Spear at L130 returned identical per-level costs — confirming the curve is weapon-type independent. Spot tests verified the fit holds across the range.

L0–L49 has no measured data; the model extrapolates. Predicted L0 = 590,292 XP. Anyone with a fresh weapon can verify by reading the cost off the in-game UI.

Why quartic?▼

Second differences of per-level costs grow with level rather than staying constant — ruling out a quadratic model.

A cubic fit produced a systematic ~2% underestimate at mid-levels (L80, L130) — implying a higher-degree term.

Quartic (degree 4) brings max residual to ±0.077% — well inside typical UI rounding. Higher degrees overfit without measurable accuracy gain on the anchor data.

Anyone wanting to verify can pull a weapon's cost from the in-game UI at any unfitted level and compare. Discrepancies welcome — submit an issue and the coefficients will be re-fit.
